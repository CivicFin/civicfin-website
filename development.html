What these figures mean — scope, sources and limits

Counted, with boundaries

1,205 tests: the reported, accepted full regression run on the fixture-policy build, subsequently merged with identical content. The four-test increase comes from test-helper shutdown checks. This page did not rerun the CARM suite.

15 human scenarios: eight Step 4 scenarios and seven A1 scenarios passed by Mark on Windows. Setup, shutdown, repeats and developer browser checks are excluded.

Five increments: the reviewed customer-creation storage, gateway, entry/review, save and name-prefill changes. This is not a count of all completed product features.

Test data, not customer claims

The 1,000 customers and 175 service accounts are synthetic fixture records, not real users, production volumes or proof of scale.

Evidence comes from accepted review records, Windows UAT results and the repository's documented test dataset. Private source records remain private.

No cumulative execution total, coverage percentage, resolved-defect total or transaction-throughput claim is published. None has a reliable reconciled measure for this snapshot.
