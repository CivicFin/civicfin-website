Issues & approach

What are your issues?

Good collections software starts with the work. These are the operational problems shaping CARM's design — not claims that every capability below has already been delivered.

01

Are teams spending time checking accounts that don't actually need human attention?

Large portfolios can create large queues. But volume isn't the same as value.

How we approach it: Design routine assessment around meaningful changes in the account, with a clear reason for bringing a case to a person. Broader automated assessment remains future work.
02

Do customers receive fragmented or overlapping collection communications?

Activity centred on individual transactions can make the wider customer experience difficult to understand.

How we approach it: Keep individual financial records accurate while designing the collection strategy around the account's current circumstances. Communications need to follow that context.
03

Do disputes, arrangements or changing circumstances make workflows difficult to manage?

Real accounts rarely follow a neat, linear path. Circumstances change.

How we approach it: Design around current account context, governed rules and meaningful events rather than forcing every case down one fixed ladder.
04

Are skilled staff doing work a system could safely do for them?

Waiting, checking, calculating, routing and routine administration consume valuable time.

How we approach it: Build controlled, testable workflow for repeatable steps. Keep people responsible for acceptance and decisions that need judgement; do not treat automation as authority to do more.
05

Does changing policy mean changing the software?

Authorities legitimately differ in policy, thresholds, approvals and operating practice.

How we approach it: Keep policy, authority and source-system responsibilities explicit. Design configuration within controlled boundaries rather than promise that every requirement is already configurable.
Your turn

Those aren't your biggest problems?

Good. Tell us what is. CivicFin should be shaped by real operational friction, not assumptions made in a software bubble.
