The mission

Better collections shouldn't mean more chasing.

Local authorities manage complex debt in complex human circumstances. CivicFin's mission is to make collections more intelligent, transparent and manageable — for the teams doing the work and the residents experiencing it.

01 / DECISIONS

Focus on what matters

Help teams identify when action is useful, when circumstances have changed and when doing nothing is the right decision.

02 / WORKFLOW

Make work make sense

Reduce avoidable administration and direct people towards cases where judgement, conversation and experience add value.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Create greater clarity

Keep the financial picture and collection journey understandable, consistent and easier to navigate.

Our point of view
Built for how collections should work — not how legacy technology made them work.
What we're building

A modern collections platform for local government.

CivicFin is being developed from first principles around the realities of public-service collections. The product is currently in development, and we're deliberately keeping the clever machinery behind it under wraps for now.

What we're not doing

Technology for technology's sake.

No magic-AI promises. No pretending every case is the same. No fake claims about a product that hasn't been built yet. The aim is practical technology, strong governance and better operational outcomes.
