The mission

Better collections shouldn't mean more chasing.

Local authorities manage complex debt in complex human circumstances. Our aim is to make the financial position clear, keep collection decisions traceable and help teams focus their effort — while recognising the circumstances of the people behind the balances.

01 / DECISIONS

Focus on what matters

Design around the account's current circumstances: what changed, what needs attention and why an action is appropriate.

02 / WORKFLOW

Make work make sense

Make responsibilities and approval steps clear. Bring useful work to the right person instead of relying on another report to chase.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Create greater clarity

Keep financial records, account balances and the collection journey understandable, without confusing the money owed with how a case is treated.

Our point of view
Financial truth first. Collection decisions with context.
What we're building

CARM. A platform taking shape.

The prototype already brings together customer and account records, invoice entry and approvals, customer search and governed customer creation. Each increment is tested and accepted before it progresses. CARM remains in development.

What we're not doing

Technology for technology's sake.

Practical software, clear responsibilities and evidence before claims. We separate what the prototype demonstrates today from what still needs design and delivery. Outcomes will need to be measured in real use.
