Direction / Not yet delivered

Work with the systems.
Remove the blockers.

CARM's design direction is to support both its own receivables and financial records owned elsewhere. Clear ownership matters more than pretending every organisation starts from the same place.

NATIVE RECEIVABLES

CARM owns the invoices

The architectural model allows CARM to create and maintain receivables. The full provisioning and account lifecycle remain design work.

EXTERNALLY MANAGED RECEIVABLES

CARM manages collections

Another financial system owns the records. Invoice interfaces, import contracts and reconciliation must be designed and tested before integration is claimed.

Designed for easier adoption, evolution and exit.

An aim, not a promise of effortless implementation. Data ownership, migration routes, configuration and the practicalities of leaving all need explicit design.

A staged direction, not a delivery promise
